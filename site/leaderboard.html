How to interpret the scores

Functional evaluation

Native geometry and required operations determine success. Reconstruction includes metric centre checks; motion includes continuous kinematic checks. Missing dimensions are NA.

Auxiliary studies

Astra supplies visual ratings, not participant results. Qwen3.5:9B supplies a separate best-of-four probe, not a frontier leaderboard entry or an RL-training claim.

Reward calibration, variability and optimization evidence

Calibration covers 30 instances and 360 repeated scores. Same-output numerical rescoring, five paired native replays, independent-request variation and repeated visual judgments are distinct studies.

The Qwen probe contains ten pools and forty candidates. Full measurements, limits and exploratory extensions are retained in the paper and structured evidence.
