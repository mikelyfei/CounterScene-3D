\paragraph{Extended learning diagnostics.}
Uniform random selection in the original pools yields mean hidden quality 0.075, compared with 0.20 after public-reward selection. Gains are concentrated in A. Five completed feedback/view controls add behavioral context: GPT solves B-L2-s0 with execution-only feedback where its structured/scalar runs do not, while its fixed-view C-L2-s0 run succeeds in 10 calls versus 26 with active access. These post-hoc single-case comparisons motivate larger matched panels; one Claude C continuation is administratively incomplete and excluded.

Eight matched-budget arms cover C, D, two-operation A and E: independent proposals versus public-feedback refinement, three proposals per stage. A public-only JSON adapter produces programs without target answers. Twenty-six of 30 proposals enter native scoring, separating interface competence from geometric planning. All eight episodes remain below strict completion; 29 proposals receive zero admissible reward, identifying a curriculum and feedback-density opportunity.

Two D studies directly expose actual shaped reward, each reusing a starter and adding two generations. One starts from the matched first proposal; the other from the first publicly executable own proposal. Neither increases reward or reaches strict completion. A separate safe D candidate receives partial reward 0.4 without completing transfer. These distinctions make progress signals inspectable and inform staged RL investigation; training benefits require the expanded experiments below.
