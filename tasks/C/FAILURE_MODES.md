# Task C: failure modes and reward attacks

These are constructed attack mechanisms and observed controls, not a claim of universal exploit resistance. Model-specific empirical failures are reported separately in the comparative results.

## program and asset identity

Classification: hard-to-hack. Scope: harder_to_hack_in_tested_domain.

Attack: Empty/malformed or substituted outputs.

Evidence: All 30 empty-output controls have zero strict and shaped reward; fixed catalogue/schema/interpreter gates prevent arbitrary asset replacement.

Mitigation: Bounded interpreter, complete instance coverage, immutable asset identity.

Limit: Not a proof of interpreter security; invalid-output fixtures do not exhaust possible programs.

## native physics and contact

Classification: hackable-but-bounded. Scope: bounded_geometric_guard.

Attack: Keep stale collisions or exploit contact tolerance.

Evidence: Native faults remain in private geometry even where misleading feedback hides them; calibration gates withhold unsafe credit.

Mitigation: Actual mesh checks, explicit support/contact rules, fail-closed unknown evidence.

Limit: Numerical/contact tolerances, conservative enclosures and absent dynamics bound the claim.

## metric visual grounding

Classification: hackable-but-bounded. Scope: visual_proxy_is_hackable.

Attack: Obtain plausible silhouettes with displaced small objects.

Evidence: C intermediates and retained model failures separate silhouette IoU from per-object metric centre correctness.

Mitigation: Conjunctive hidden-view and every-object centre checks.

Limit: Coverage is a ray-visibility heuristic and does not prove a unique inverse reconstruction.

## minimal edit and preservation

Classification: hackable-but-bounded. Scope: edit_cost_alone_is_hackable.

Attack: Keep the input unchanged to minimize movement.

Evidence: Stale/unchanged calibration controls expose that low edit cost can accompany task failure.

Mitigation: Report edit cost separately; enforce active goals and locked preservation before interpreting efficiency.

Limit: The scalar shaped average is not a universal utility function.

## auxiliary visual quality

Classification: soft/vibes. Scope: soft_visual_evidence.

Attack: Exploit occlusion or visually plausible but invalid geometry.

Evidence: Fixed-image Astra repeats and calibration are a separate pending/terminal study; no result is inferred from deterministic calibration.

Mitigation: Anonymous rubric, uncertain=NA, report coverage and disagreement with primary scoring.

Limit: Stochastic judge and same-provider bias; static views cannot certify metric or motion validity.

## reward or target leakage

Classification: hackable-but-bounded. Scope: bounded_interface_separation.

Attack: Read hidden targets or optimize hidden score directly.

Evidence: Participant inputs exclude evaluator files; the balanced small-model probe selects by public tools and audits hidden quality afterward.

Mitigation: Restricted public parameters, package separation, exact retained public histories.

Limit: Development templates are known and are not held-out adversarial security tests.
